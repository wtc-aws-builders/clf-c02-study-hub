# Free study resources for CLF-C02

Everything you need to pass the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam is free.
This page is the one place to find it. Each week's issue tells you which part to study that week.

You need a free **AWS Skill Builder** account for most of these: sign in at [skillbuilder.aws](https://skillbuilder.aws/)
and use the search box to find each course by its name below.

## Practice exam options, from free to paid

1. Our Study Hub quiz (free, grows every week, exam mix mode weighted like the real exam)
2. The knowledge checks in Cloud Practitioner Essentials (free)
3. The Official Practice Question Set (free, 20 questions)
4. The Official Pretest (65 questions, 90 minutes): check whether it shows as free on your Skill Builder account

**Never use exam dump sites** or anything claiming to have real exam questions. Using or sharing real exam content
breaks the AWS Certification Program Agreement and can get your certificate revoked. Their answers are often wrong too.

You do not need to pay for anything to pass. If a resource asks for payment, check this page for the free one first.

## Hands-on AWS without spending money

- **Own AWS account:** choose the **Free plan** when signing up. It gives credits and cannot charge your card,
  but sign-up still asks for a card to verify you.
- **No card?** Use AWS Educate labs. Every weekly practical has a sandbox or no-account path that counts the same.
- Read [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md) before you touch AWS.
